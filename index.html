Distributed E-Voting System

A fault-tolerant, decentralized electronic voting architecture designed to eliminate single points of failure. Combines WebAuthn biometric passwordless authentication with Ethereum smart contract immutability to ensure verifiable and tamper-proof elections.

Key Technical Highlights:
